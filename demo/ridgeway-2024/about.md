**Everything in this archive is invented.** There is no Ridgeway Commons
Trust and nobody named in these pages; the collection is a worked example
built by `demo/build-demo.py` in the [Stackroom
repository](https://github.com/igor-vuta/stackroom), published so that the
tool can be demonstrated on a disclosure that hurts nobody.

It is the smaller of the two demo collections, and it exists for one reason:
Stackroom reads one jurisdiction's exemption vocabulary per collection, and
this one is stamped with sections of the UK Freedom of Information Act 2000 —
s.36(2), s.40(2) and s.43(2) — where the [main demo](../) cites US FOIA and
Privacy Act codes.
